Join us for a technical walkthrough of modern voice agent architectures, grounded in a customer scenario involving conversational AI for incoming and outgoing customer calls. Voice agents are quickly moving from demos to real customer scenarios, but choosing the right architecture requires understanding much more than the model itself.
 
We’ll compare cascaded architectures, where speech-to-text, an LLM, and text-to-speech work together as a pipeline, with end-to-end speech-to-speech approaches that process and generate audio directly. Next, we'll explore Voice Agents in Foundry and how they compare to other voice solutions. A few live demonstrations will drive home the main concepts.
 
**Agenda:**
1. Overview of Voice Use-cases
2. Cascaded versus speech-to-speech architectures
3. Foundry Voice Agent fundamentals and conversational flow
4. Architecture trade-offs and solutioning considerations
5. Live voice agent demo
 
Attendees will leave with a clearer understanding of the voice AI landscape and a practical way to evaluate architecture choices based on a customer’s actual requirements rather than simply choosing a model or technology.
